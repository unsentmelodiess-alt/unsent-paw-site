# Design directions

## Approach 1 — Fireside Editorial
**Very Brief Intro:** A quiet, editorial sanctuary with creamy paper textures, moss-green accents, and intimate photographic vignettes. It feels like a well-loved remembrance journal rather than a social feed.

**Probability:** 0.07

## Approach 2 — Moonlit Listening Room
**Very Brief Intro:** A night-first listening space using deep ink, amber light, and a soft audio-console sensibility. The experience privileges calm rituals and slow, focused moments.

**Probability:** 0.04

## Approach 3 — Gentle Field Notes
**Very Brief Intro:** A tactile notebook-inspired site with warm natural colors, animal traces, and modular content cards. It balances emotional comfort with practical dog-behavior learning.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen approach — Fireside Editorial

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial wellness design with subtle craft-paper tactility and the still-life warmth of a private remembrance journal.

### Core Principles
1. Give quiet moments generous room instead of filling every surface.
2. Use a clear editorial hierarchy: emotive display type for reflection and plain, readable body copy for support.
3. Make every interaction feel gentle and deliberate, with soft elevated surfaces rather than sharp visual noise.
4. Keep the emotional content dignified: no gamified grief, no urgent selling, and no artificial testimonial language.

### Color Philosophy
Cream is the resting place for the eye; muted sage signals steadiness; bark brown supplies grounding; and a small amount of rusted apricot introduces life and warmth. Dark mode becomes a charcoal-and-ink listening room, not an inverted version of the daylight site.

### Layout Paradigm
The homepage reads like a long editorial spread with a narrow vertical rail for listening controls and a staggered sequence of feature panels. It avoids a generic centered landing-page stack by shifting content blocks and allowing full-bleed visual moments to alternate with text-led quiet zones.

### Signature Elements
1. A hand-drawn paw-and-soundwave mark.
2. Fine curved contour lines that resemble both topographic maps and sleeping-pet breathing rhythms.
3. Rounded archival photo frames paired with small paper-label captions.

### Interaction Philosophy
Buttons use clear, intimate verbs such as “Leave a memory” and “Stay awhile.” Cards lift slightly and reveal a subtle underline or audio state; the experience never uses aggressive motion or conversion pressure.

### Animation
Transitions use a 180–240ms soft ease-out. Sections rise a few pixels with opacity on entry, but respect reduced-motion settings. The audio player uses a slow waveform shimmer only while playing.

### Typography System
Fraunces is the expressive display voice for headings and memory excerpts; Manrope is the pragmatic sans-serif for navigation, forms, and body copy. Large headings use tight tracking and mixed case; body text stays open and highly legible.

### Brand Essence
**A gentle digital home for the music, memories, and everyday understanding that bind people to their pets.**

Warm, observant, dignified.

### Brand Voice
Headlines should sound reflective and specific; CTAs should be invitational and unhurried. Examples: “Some love keeps echoing.” and “A place to leave the little things you still remember.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact, hand-drawn paw print emerging from a single continuous musical line. The working wordmark is intentionally stored in one config file so the final brand name can replace it without reworking the interface.

### Signature Brand Color
**Hearth Moss — #75836D**

## Style Decisions

- The hand-drawn continuous paw-and-soundwave line is the recurring identity mark across the wordmark, listening controls, editorial stories, and product shelf.
- The homepage uses a visible vertical listening rail and staggered editorial pacing rather than an even card-first landing-page pattern.
- Images are framed as rounded archival “found memories,” with small paper-label captions and still-life detail rather than generic lifestyle imagery.
